*Navcam* is short for Navigation Camera. It's a normal wide-angle perspective, not a fisheye. The frames here are the raw feed's 1200-pixel-wide versions of a 5120 x 3840 sensor, so fine detail is lost; the full-resolution products are on the NASA raw-images site. Expect a wide color landscape, often one tile of a larger panorama; frames sharing a sequence id were taken together as a mosaic.

## WATSON on Perseverance rover

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/2026-10-14/perseverance-SI1_2008_0845210693_527ECM_N0911496SRLC00354_0000LMJ.jpg' | absolute_url }}" alt="Perseverance SHERLOC_WATSON, sol 2008, filter OPEN">

*WATSON* is short for Wide Angle Topographic Sensor for Operations and eNgineering. A 1600x1200-pixel color sensor behind a fixed macro lens with no optical zoom; instead the arm moves the whole camera to change scale, focusing from about 2cm off the surface out to infinity. Close up it resolves roughly 14 micrometers per pixel, coarsening to around 100 micrometers per pixel farther back. Close-up, well-lit color shots of rock or soil texture, sometimes taken as a stack at slightly different distances or as a small mosaic; wider, more distant shots are usually rover selfies or arm/instrument inspections rather than science targets.
