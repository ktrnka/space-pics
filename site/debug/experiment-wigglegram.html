Wigglegram experiment

Left and right frames of the same tile from a stereo camera pair, taken at the same instant, alternated. The two frames are shifted to line up the centre of the scene (the subject), so things nearer or farther than the subject wobble and the eye reads depth. Pairs that align poorly alternate more slowly. No 3D glasses, an old trick. explorer index
